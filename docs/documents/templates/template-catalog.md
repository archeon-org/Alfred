# Template Catalog

This is the human-readable catalog of seeded template packs from `apps/gate/db/templates.json`.

## 1) General Life

### Root folders
- Inbox: To Classify, Action Required, Waiting Response
- Identity & Legal: ID & Passport, Contracts, Official Letters, Certificates
- Finance: Bills, Bank Statements, Taxes, Insurance Payments, Receipts
- Home & Utilities: Rent & Mortgage, Utility Bills, Home Maintenance, Home Insurance
- Health: Medical Reports, Prescriptions, Lab Results, Insurance Claims
- Mobility: Vehicle Documents, Maintenance, Fines & Claims, Transport Subscriptions
- Work Admin: Employment Contracts, Payslips, HR Letters
- Travel: Bookings, Visas, Claims
- Archive: Current Year, Previous Years, Permanent Records

## 2) Freelancer Pro

### Root folders
- Leads & Clients: Prospects, Active Clients, Past Clients
- Contracts & SOW: Draft Contracts, Signed Contracts, Amendments
- Proposals & Pitches: Proposals Sent, Follow-ups, Won/Lost Deals
- Invoicing: Draft Invoices, Sent Invoices, Paid Invoices, Overdue Invoices, Credit Notes
- Expenses: Software & Subscriptions, Equipment, Travel, Subcontractors
- Tax & Compliance: VAT/Sales Tax, Income Tax, Accountant Exchanges, Business Registrations
- Delivery & QA: Work In Progress, Final Deliverables, Revisions, Client Feedback
- Marketing & Brand: Portfolio, Case Studies, Social Content
- Operations: SOPs, Legal, Banking
- Archive: Closed Projects, Yearly Archive

## 3) Small Business Ops

### Root folders
- Corporate: Incorporation, Board Documents, Shareholder Records
- Accounting: Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Reconciliations, Financial Statements
- Tax & Payroll: Payroll, Tax Filings, Social Contributions, Employee Declarations
- Human Resources: Hiring, Contracts, Employee Records, Performance
- Sales: Quotes, Orders, Contracts, Renewals
- Procurement: Vendors, Purchase Orders, Delivery Notes
- Legal & Risk: Policies, Insurance, Compliance Audits, Incidents
- IT & Security: Software Licenses, Access Logs, Security Policies, Vendor Contracts
- Archive: Fiscal Years, Closed Contracts, Historical HR

## 4) Student & Research

### Root folders
- Admissions & Enrollment: Applications, Enrollment Proof, Admin Correspondence
- Coursework: Syllabi, Lecture Notes, Assignments, Projects
- Exams & Grades: Exam Prep, Exam Papers, Grades & Transcripts
- Research: Literature, Research Notes, Experiments & Data, Draft Papers
- Thesis: Proposal, Writing Drafts, Supervisor Feedback, Final Submission
- Internships & Career: CV & Cover Letters, Applications, Interviews, Offers & Contracts
- Student Finance: Tuition, Scholarships, Housing, Living Expenses
- International: Visa, Residence Permit, Insurance, Travel
- Archive: Semesters, Completed Courses, Graduated

## 5) Family Office

### Root folders
- Adults: Identity, Employment, Banking, Legal
- Children: School, Medical, Activities, Guardianship
- Household Finance: Bills, Loans, Taxes, Budget Reports
- Housing: Lease & Mortgage, Utilities, Renovation, Property Insurance
- Vehicles: Registrations, Insurance, Maintenance, Claims & Fines
- Health & Medical: Medical Records, Prescriptions, Vaccines, Claims
- Education & Childcare: School Contracts, Reports, Fees
- Estate Planning: Wills, Power of Attorney, Notary
- Archive: Yearly Archive, Permanent Documents

## 6) Landlord & Real Estate

### Root folders
- Acquisition: Offers, Notary Documents, Financing, Due Diligence
- Tenants: Applications, Signed Leases, Deposits, Move In/Out
- Rent & Billing: Rent Receipts, Arrears, Charges, Utility Rebilling
- Maintenance: Planned Works, Incidents, Vendor Invoices, Warranties
- Legal & Compliance: Regulatory Certificates, Insurance, Disputes, Eviction Cases
- Accounting & Tax: Expense Reports, Income Statements, Tax Returns, Accountant Exchange
- Vendors: Contracts, Quotes, Work Orders
- Communication: Tenant Communication, HOA/Building, Authorities
- Archive: By Property, Closed Tenancies, Previous Fiscal Years

## 7) Healthcare & Medical

### Root folders
- Personal Health Profile: Identity, Medical History, Allergies & Alerts, Emergency Contacts
- Consultations: General Practitioner, Specialists, Visit Summaries
- Diagnostics: Lab Results, Imaging, Pathology
- Treatment: Prescriptions, Treatment Plans, Hospitalization, Rehabilitation
- Insurance: Policies, Claims, Pre-Authorizations, Reimbursements
- Billing: Invoices, Payment Proofs, Outstanding
- Family Medical: Partner, Children, Parents
- Preventive Care: Vaccines, Screenings, Annual Checkups
- Archive: Yearly Archive, Long-Term Records

## 8) Travel & Immigration

### Root folders
- Identity & Citizenship: Passports, National IDs, Civil Status, Consular Docs
- Immigration Cases: Visa Applications, Residence Permits, Work Permits, Renewals
- Employment Abroad: Contracts, Tax Number Docs, Payroll
- Housing Abroad: Lease Contracts, Utility Contracts, Deposit & Move Docs
- Tax & Banking: Foreign Tax Filings, Bank Statements, Compliance Letters
- Education Abroad: Admission, Enrollment, Certificates
- Travel Operations: Bookings, Itineraries, Claims
- Legal & Translation: Certified Translations, Apostille, Attorney Docs
- Archive: Closed Cases, Previous Visas, Permanent Records

## Notes

- All templates use a strict two-level folder hierarchy.
- Child folders are intended as the preferred AI classification targets.
- Archive folders are included in every pack for lifecycle management.
